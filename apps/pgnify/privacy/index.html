Pgnify: Chess Analysis & Stats

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Restart Apps ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Pgnify: Chess Analysis & Stats (the "App"). Pgnify provides educational chess analysis, saved game reviews, and public player information. The App is intended for adults age 18 and older.

Pgnify does not create an app account and does not provide app-operated cloud sync. Chess analysis and coaching explanations run locally on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

The App can store your public Chess.com username, preferences, imported games, engine evidence, coaching reviews, favorite players, checked player games, and earned review-unlock receipts in private app storage. Pgnify does not ask for your Chess.com password.

Android app backup and device transfer are disabled. On iOS, local games, reviews, settings, and unlock receipts may be included in an operating-system device backup. Removing the App or clearing its local data removes locally saved information and earned unlock receipts, although restoring an iOS device backup may restore a local copy.

3. Chess.com game data

When you check recent games, Pgnify sends the public username you entered and a network request to Chess.com's public API. Chess.com returns public games, player information, and related metadata under its own terms and privacy practices.

Checks occur on demand, when relevant player screens open, or during startup and foreground checks when you enable that preference for your own username. Pgnify does not promise instant synchronization and does not monitor players in the background for notifications. See Chess.com's Privacy Policy.

4. PGN imports and local analysis

Importing a file uses your operating system's document picker. If you choose a file from a cloud document provider, that provider may download the selected file to your device. Pgnify copies the file text into an import draft and saves it after valid PGN processing.

The bundled Arasan engine performs chess analysis locally. Pgnify does not use a language model or remote explanation service, and does not send your PGN text, games, engine evidence, or coaching reviews in advertising requests.

5. Public player information and portraits

The player directory displays dated public information such as FIDE titles, federation, gender, and ratings. Player details and portraits may come from FIDE, Wikidata, Wikimedia, Chess.com, and linked public websites. Portrait attribution and available license links are shown in the App.

When a visible portrait is fetched, Wikimedia or Chess.com's image service receives the image request and ordinary network information such as your IP address. These requests do not include your imported games or Pgnify username setting. Images may be cached locally. See Wikimedia's Privacy Policy and Chess.com's Privacy Policy.

6. Rewarded advertising and consent

Pgnify may offer an optional Google AdMob rewarded ad to unlock a full review for a selected game. Google's AdMob and User Messaging Platform SDKs may process device and advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, device information, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, consent, and fraud prevention. Depending on your region and choices, an ad may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, the App presents Google's native consent message and a privacy-options entry in Settings. The current App does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not send a custom user identifier to the advertising SDK. This does not mean third-party SDK collection is absent. Review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob data guidance.

7. Analytics, diagnostics, and Firebase

Pgnify does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or another Firebase runtime SDK. Firebase may be used by Restart Apps to distribute beta builds, but that build-distribution process is not an App runtime service and does not provide app accounts or cloud sync.

8. Permissions and third-party links

Internet access is used for public game data, public player information, portraits, links, advertising, and consent choices. The document picker is used only when you select a PGN file. Pgnify does not request location, contacts, or broad device-storage permission.

Social, reference, attribution, and player-site links can open in your browser or another app. The destination service's terms and privacy policy apply.

9. Your choices and retention

You can edit your public username and preferences, remove locally saved content using available App controls, change advertising privacy choices when that option is shown, or remove the App to delete its local data. Pgnify has no account-deletion flow because it does not create user accounts or maintain an app-operated cloud account.

10. Security and age eligibility

We rely on platform storage protections and HTTPS for supported network requests, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in the United States or other countries under their own safeguards.

Pgnify is intended only for adults age 18 and older. We do not knowingly seek personal information from anyone under 18 through the App.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. We will revise the effective date shown above and provide additional notice when required.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Restart Apps at restartapplication@gmail.com.
