Pgnify Support

For help with Pgnify, email us with your device model (iPhone, iPad, or Android device), its system version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not include passwords or sensitive information.

Email support

Game imports and analysis

Include whether the game came from a public Chess.com username, pasted PGN, or a PGN file. A screenshot and the step where analysis stopped are useful, but do not send a Chess.com password because Pgnify never needs one.

Practice and progress

Practice positions and the accuracy and weakness summaries on Home come from your own games whose review you have unlocked at least once. If they are missing, analyze one of your games and open its review. Practice answers come from each game's saved analysis; a move the engine rates within 0.3 pawns of its first choice also counts.

Privacy choices

If a game review does not open, include your region, whether a privacy message appeared, and what the App showed. Where Google requires it, advertising privacy choices can be revisited in Pgnify Settings. Usage analytics and crash reports can be turned off at any time in Settings.

Local data

Pgnify has no app account or cloud sync. Removing the App or clearing its data removes locally saved games, reviews, favorites, settings, and earned unlock receipts. Restart Apps cannot restore local data from a server.
